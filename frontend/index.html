About Our Platform

The Farmer Query Support System is a comprehensive AI-powered agricultural platform designed to provide Indian farmers with instant access to expert advice, market information, and government schemes. Our mission is to bridge the gap between farmers and agricultural expertise, enabling better decision-making for improved crop yields and sustainable farming practices.
