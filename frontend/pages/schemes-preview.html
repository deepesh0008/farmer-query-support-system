Scheme Details Example

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

Benefits

  • ₹6,000 per year to all eligible farmers
  • Distributed in 3 installments of ₹2,000
  • Direct transfer to bank account
  • No hidden charges or paperwork

Requirements

  • Indian farmer (citizen)
  • Age above 18 years
  • Active bank account
  • Valid Aadhar/ID proof
Eligibility Check

You likely qualify if you are a farmer with land records in your name or as co-owner.

How to Apply

  1. Visit your nearest agricultural office or Common Service Center
  2. Submit your Aadhar number and bank details
  3. Verify your land records
  4. Receive confirmation SMS on your registered phone
  5. Get ₹2,000 within 2-3 weeks in your bank account
