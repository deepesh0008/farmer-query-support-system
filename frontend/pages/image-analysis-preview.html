Affected Area: ~30% of leaf surface
Recommended Treatment:
1. Spray sulfur powder or fungicide (e.g., Tridemorph)
2. Ensure proper air circulation
3. Avoid overhead irrigation

Prevention for Next Season: Use resistant varieties, proper field sanitation

Tips for Best Results

DO's

  • Use clear, natural lighting
  • Get close-up shots of affected area
  • Include healthy parts for comparison
  • Take multiple angles if possible
  • Ensure the entire leaf is visible

DON'Ts

  • Don't use flash photography
  • Don't take photos in shadows
  • Don't crop important parts
  • Don't use heavily filtered images
  • Don't mix different plants in one image
