📸 Photography Tips for Best Results

✓ Do's:

  • Take clear, well-lit photos
  • Focus on the affected area
  • Include crop name in description
  • Capture multiple angles if possible
  • Use natural daylight

✗ Don'ts:

  • Avoid blurry or dark photos
  • Don't crop out the problem area
  • Avoid shadows or backlighting
  • Don't take photos with flash
  • Avoid photos from too far away
